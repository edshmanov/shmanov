No heat? Check these first

Sometimes the fix takes a minute. If none of these help, give us a call.

  1. Check the thermostat

    Make sure it's set to Heat and a few degrees above the room temperature. Replace the batteries if the screen is blank.

  2. Change the filter

    A clogged filter can make the furnace overheat and shut itself off.

  3. Check the power

    Look for a tripped breaker, and make sure the switch near the furnace is on.

  4. Still nothing? Call us

    Tell us what the furnace is doing and we'll take it from there.

Smell gas?

Don't use switches or phones inside. Leave the house first, then call your gas company or 911.
