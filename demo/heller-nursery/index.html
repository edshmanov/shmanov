Three generations of growing

From a backyard to one of the area's go-to greenhouses, still run by the Heller family.

  1. 1946

    Bobby Heller starts growing in his backyard.

  2. 1950

    The nursery moves to Peterson, Indiana.

  3. 1988

    His son John Heller joins and goes on to run the nursery.

  4. Today

    Jessie (Heller) Myers, the third generation, helps run the business with her family.
